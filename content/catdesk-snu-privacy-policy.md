# CatDesk SNU Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-27

## Scope

CatDesk SNU is a personal-use connector operated by its developer, ChungSeokHan. It connects ChatGPT to a CatDesk service running on the operator's own computer. This policy covers that connector and service, not OpenAI, ChatGPT, GitHub, ngrok, or other third-party services, each of which has its own privacy terms.

## Information processed

When the connector is used, the service may process the request content, selected tool arguments, and tool results needed to perform the requested action. It may also process operational metadata such as timestamps, error messages, and service health information.

## How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate and process connector requests, execute the requested tool action, troubleshoot failures, and maintain service security. The connector is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information.

## Storage, sharing, and retention

The service is designed to operate on the operator's own computer. Requests are relayed through an encrypted ngrok endpoint to that computer. The developer does not operate a separate central database for connector requests.

Operational records, if generated, remain in the operator-controlled local environment and are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, security, or legal obligations. The operator may delete local records subject to those needs.

Information is not sold. It is not intentionally shared with third parties except when required to deliver the connector through the services involved in a request (such as OpenAI/ChatGPT and ngrok), when the operator directs it, or when required by law.

## Security

The CatDesk service is configured to listen locally, with access exposed through an authenticated or controlled connector path. No system can guarantee absolute security; users should avoid supplying secrets or sensitive information unless necessary and authorized.

## Your choices and contact

Use of this connector is optional. The operator can stop using it or request deletion of locally retained operational data by contacting the developer through the [project repository](https://github.com/01chungee10snu/01chungee10snu.github.io).

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the connector's operation changes. The effective date above will be updated with material changes.
