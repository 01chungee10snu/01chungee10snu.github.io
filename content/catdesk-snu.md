# CatDesk SNU

CatDesk SNU is a personal-use ChatGPT connector that lets its operator request approved actions from a CatDesk service running on the operator's own computer.

It is intended for controlled computer automation and tool execution within the operator's ChatGPT account. It is not a public consumer service and does not offer purchases or subscriptions.
